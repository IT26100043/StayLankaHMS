About Us

Nelum Retreat is a small hotel set among gardens in Colombo 07. Every stay is built around simple things done well: a comfortable bed, a proper garden breakfast, a rooftop pool and a front desk that never closes. Our concierge can arrange airport transfers, train tickets and restaurant tables, so you only need to ask once.
